\section{Mechanisation}\label{sec:mechanisation}

The development is about 6{,}000 lines of Rocq 9.0.1, available at
\url{\repobase}, using Mathematical Components~2.5.0~\cite{mathcomp} with
Hierarchy Builder~1.10.1, deriving~0.2.2 and, for the coinductive definitions of
the original paper, paco~4.2.3~\cite{paco} (\cref{sec:development}). Continuous
integration rebuilds it on every change and checks that the paper's results
assume nothing beyond the two parameters that fix the security lattice. Identifiers
in typewriter font in this paper link to their definitions.

\paragraph{Types} Input and output types range over an inductive universe \m{Ty} of
codes, interpreted into \code{Set}, rather than over \code{Set} directly. This makes
reductions invertible, which the negative result needs: refuting non-interference
means showing that a trace is \emph{not} admitted.

\paragraph{Axioms} The development assumes neither classical logic nor uniqueness of
identity proofs, which dependent elimination on the type-indexed reductions would
need; we invert reductions through their determinism, one lemma per rule (\m{inv_mapO}
and its siblings).

\paragraph{Departures} Our definitions follow Rafnsson et al., with one essential
departure: the original defines non-interference coinductively over infinite streams,
and we quantify over finite traces (\cref{def:ni}), which makes every proof in the
development an induction on a trace. \m{NI_paper} proves the two equivalent, so
\cref{thm:leak,thm:main} also hold as the original reads them, hypotheses included
(\m{model_immediate_not_NI_paper}, \m{model_sliced_NI_paper};
\cref{sec:departures}). The corollaries are stated only in our terms. The paper's sum
notation is proved to agree with the mechanised forms (\cref{sec:agreement}).

\paragraph{Scope} \Cref{thm:leak} is a counterexample on the concrete system. The
development fixes the shape of the pool: six slots, with one scheduler, two user
processes and three handlers, and the two interrupts. The listings in this paper are
typed by hand from the Rocq sources, to which their identifiers link.

\paragraph{Figures} The runs drawn in \cref{fig:leak,fig:run-ordinary,fig:run-sliced}
are proved traces of the models, and the state rows of
\cref{fig:run-ordinary,fig:run-sliced} are computed by \m{state_step} and
stated as lemmas (\m{immediate_states_with_dI}, \m{sliced_states_with_dI} and their
siblings), which the figures show. \m{run_states_model} shows
that these are the states the model holds along those runs.
