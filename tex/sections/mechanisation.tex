\paragraph{Types} Input and output types range over an inductive universe \m{Ty} of
codes, interpreted into \code{Set}, rather than over \code{Set} directly. This makes
reductions invertible, which the negative result needs: refuting non-interference
means showing that a trace is \emph{not} admitted.

\paragraph{Axioms} \Cref{thm:leak} rests on \code{eq_rect_eq} and on the two
parameters that fix the security lattice; \cref{thm:main} and its corollary also
use excluded middle, in exactly one place. A premise of \m{swi_NI} case-splits on
awareness, which is not constructively decidable in general; a decision procedure
for it would remove the axiom. \Cref{thm:noop} uses no axioms.

\subsection{Departures from the original definitions}\label{sec:departures}

Mechanising the calculus changed four definitions (\cref{sec:background}). For
each, the original is written out in \mechfile{theories/adequacy.v}{adequacy.v} and
related to ours. That file depends only on the definitions, nothing depends on it,
and it uses no axioms beyond the lattice parameters.

\paragraph{Characterised equivalences} The original equips each type with an
$\mathcal{L}$-equivalence over $V \cup \{\unobs\}$: the distinguished value $\unobs$
is adjoined to the values, and $x \doteq_\ell \unobs$ says that $x$ is unobservable.
We use \m{cEquiv}, which keeps the values as they are and adds unobservability as a
separate predicate, the field $\dis$, tied to the equivalence $\rel$ by the
characterisation law. \Cref{sec:background} writes $x \doteq_\ell \unobs$ for
$\dis$. \m{NI_lEquiv} shows that the two give the same notion of non-interference.

\paragraph{Closure at a position} The original defines non-interference as a
coinductive simulation whose four clauses apply at the head of a stream. We state
three closure clauses that apply at an arbitrary position. \m{PNI_of_SNI} and
\m{SNI_of_PNI} prove the two equivalent.

\paragraph{Finite traces} The original quantifies over infinite streams of labels;
we quantify over finite traces. \m{NI_SNI} proves the two equivalent. It rests on
two properties: finite and infinite behaviour determine each other, which needs
reduction to be deterministic (to assemble finite runs into one infinite run) and
never to block (to continue a finite run); and insertion commutes with taking prefixes.
Composed with the previous result, \m{NI_paper} states that the original definition
and ours hold of the same processes.

\paragraph{Obliviousness by class} The side condition of \m{swi_NI} asks that the
observer be oblivious to a process. The original reads this as every reachable
output being unobservable; we ask only that every output lie in one class.
\m{oblivious_of_ObliviousDis} shows that the original condition implies ours, and
a constant process under \m{public_equiv} satisfies ours but not the original, so
the weakening is strict. It is what lets \m{swi_NI} and the obliviousness argument
of \cref{sec:gating} avoid unobservability altogether.

The composition theorems themselves are proved directly for our definitions.
