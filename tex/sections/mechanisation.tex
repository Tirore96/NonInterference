\paragraph{Types} Input and output types range over an inductive universe \m{Ty} of
codes, interpreted into \code{Set}, rather than over \code{Set} directly. This makes
reductions invertible, which the negative result needs: refuting non-interference
means showing that a trace is \emph{not} admitted.

\paragraph{Axioms} \Cref{thm:leak} rests on \code{eq_rect_eq} and on the two
parameters that fix the security lattice; \cref{thm:main} and its corollary also
use excluded middle, in exactly one place. A premise of \m{swi_NI} case-splits on
awareness, which is not constructively decidable in general; a decision procedure
for it would remove the axiom. \Cref{thm:noop} uses no axioms.

\subsection{Departures from the original definitions}\label{sec:departures}

Mechanising the calculus changed four definitions (\cref{tab:departures}). For each,
the original is written out in \code{adequacy.v} and related to ours; that file depends
only on the definitions, nothing depends on it, and it uses no axioms beyond the
lattice parameters.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\caption{The four departures from the original paper, and the results relating
  them. Departures 2 and 3 compose: \m{NI_paper} states that the original
  definition and ours hold of the same processes.}
\label{tab:departures}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lLLl@{}}
\toprule
 & original & here & result \\
\midrule
1 & L-equivalences: an equivalence on values extended with a distinguished element
  & \m{cEquiv}: separate $\rel$ and $\dis$ fields tied by the characterisation law
  & \m{NI_lEquiv} \\
2 & a coinductive simulation, four clauses applied at the head of a stream
  & three closure clauses, at an arbitrary position
  & \m{PNI_of_SNI}, \m{SNI_of_PNI} \\
3 & infinite streams of labels & finite traces & \m{NI_SNI} \\
4 & obliviousness: every reachable output unobservable
  & every output in one class & \m{oblivious_of_ObliviousDis} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table*}

Departures 1 to 3 change nothing: the two versions hold of exactly the same
processes. Departure 1 needs no choice principle, because the original's
distinguished element is adjoined to the value set rather than chosen from it.
Departure 3 rests on two properties: finite and infinite behaviour determine each
other, which needs reduction to be deterministic (to assemble finite runs into one
infinite run) and total (to continue a finite run); and insertion commutes with
taking prefixes. Departure 4 is a strict weakening of a side condition of \m{swi_NI}:
the original condition implies ours, and a constant process under \m{public_equiv}
satisfies ours but not the original. It is the weakening that lets \m{swi_NI} and the
obliviousness argument of \cref{sec:gating} avoid unobservability altogether.

These results concern definitions. The composition theorems were proved afresh for
our definitions; the original paper's proofs were not replayed.
