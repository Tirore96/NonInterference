
Definition (trace simulation):

  p ∈ (Proc I O) l-(Rₒ)-simulates t ( p --t->Rₒ,l ) is defined inductively as follows.

   0) p ---ε->Rₒ,l
   1) p --it->Rₒ,l if ∃ p' . p --i-> p' ∧ p' --t->Rₒ,l
   2) p --ot->Rₒ,l if ∃ p', o' . p --o'-> p' ∧ o' Rₒ,ₗ o ∧ p' --t->Rₒ,l

End

  p ∈ (Proc I O) is l-(Rᵢ)-(Rₒ)-noninterfering ( p ∈ NI(l,Rᵢ,Rₒ) ), iff

   0) ∀ t, t', i, i' . i Rᵢ,ₗ i' ⇒  p --tit'->Rₒ,l  ⇒  p --ti't'->Rₒ,l

  p is (Rᵢ)-(Rₒ)-noninterfering ( p ∈ NI(Rᵢ,Rₒ) ), iff
